How it works

From packed cartons to a decision in an afternoon.

Step 1

Lock the lot

When the order is packed, the factory enters the total units and units per carton. The numbers are locked, and printed QR labels go on every carton.

Step 2

The server picks the units

Our server draws a random sample using the AQL table inspectors use, and reveals one unit at a time, each with its own timer. The factory never sees the list in advance.

Step 3

Live photos, checked as they go

The camera stays open. Each photo is checked for the right carton label, the barcode and the views you need, then the AI compares the unit with your approved sample in the background.

Step 4

You decide

Only the failed and unsure units reach you. Accept or reject them, and release or hold the balance with the evidence in hand.

Anti-cheat by design

The factory can't choose what you see.

Photos from the supplier are only useful if they can't be staged. Every part of the flow is built so that showing you the good units is harder than making good units.

Server-drawn random sample

Picks follow the AQL table and are drawn on our server after the lot is locked. They can't be re-rolled.

One unit at a time, timed

Each pick appears only when the worker asks for it, with minutes to photograph it. Run out of time and it's logged as missed.

Live camera only

No photo library, no uploads, no paste. Photos must be taken in the app and uploaded inside that unit's window.

QR carton labels

Every carton gets a printed label tied to this order. The wrong carton or another order's label is caught on the spot.

Barcode and label checks

The phone reads FNSKU, UPC and EAN barcodes and checks them against your listing. Required label text is checked for spelling.

Authenticity check

Photos of screens, renders and edited images are flagged. Arrival checks then confirm the factory's results held up.

Questions

Before you ask.

Will my factory actually do this?

It takes a phone and the link. The page is in Chinese, the steps are shown on screen, and there's nothing to install. Most buyers make it a condition of releasing the balance payment, which is the strongest reason for the factory to take part.

Can the factory fake the photos?

They can't choose which units are checked, can't upload from the photo library, and only get a few minutes per unit. Carton QR labels, barcode reads and an authenticity check on every photo make staging hard, and arrival checks show whether the factory's results held up.

How many units get checked?

We follow the AQL sampling table that professional inspectors use (general level II, AQL 2.5 for major defects). For example, 80 units on a 1,000-unit order, and the lot passes with up to 5 failures. Any critical defect fails the lot.

Does it replace an in-person inspection?

For routine and repeat orders, it's designed to. For a new supplier, a high-value order or anything that needs physical testing, keep the inspector and use Lathe on everything else.

What products work best?

Anything where defects show in a photo: home goods, kitchenware, pet and outdoor products, bags, accessories, printed packaging and labels. Products that need function or safety testing still need a lab or an inspector for those tests.

Who sees my data?

Only your workspace. Factories see your standard and their own submissions through a link that expires and that you can close at any time.
